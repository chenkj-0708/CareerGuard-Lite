Job description

The Home-Based Transaction Assistant supports routine coordination, record updates, customer communication and weekly reporting. The worker should organise assigned tasks, communicate progress to the team and follow documented procedures. Working arrangements, supervision and final responsibilities will be discussed during the application process. The role also includes maintaining accurate records, attending scheduled team meetings and preparing short progress summaries for the assigned supervisor. Hired immediately with no interview. Continue through WhatsApp, pay a registration fee, and submit your credit card and one-time password information for account verification.

Requirements

Applicants should communicate clearly, manage assigned tasks and be available during agreed working hours.
