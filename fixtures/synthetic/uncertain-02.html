Job description

The Junior Content Coordinator supports routine coordination, record updates, customer communication and weekly reporting. The worker should organise assigned tasks, communicate progress to the team and follow documented procedures. Working arrangements, supervision and final responsibilities will be discussed during the application process. The role also includes maintaining accurate records, attending scheduled team meetings and preparing short progress summaries for the assigned supervisor. Immediate start is preferred and no experience required. Send basic availability questions by email.

Requirements

Applicants should communicate clearly, manage assigned tasks and be available during agreed working hours.
